Scionos Capture lets you capture a web page, annotate it and prepare a document on your device. No account is required and screenshots are not sent to a server.

Capture a page
Choose the visible area, a selection, a scrolling region, the whole page or an element. Adjust the bounds before confirming. A cancellable delay lets you prepare a menu or a temporary page state.

Compose and annotate
Combine and reorder multiple imported or pasted images, with titles and captions. Update 1.5.1 refines source sharing, rejects oversized image files before decoding, and fixes image processing and printing. The editor provides drawing, text, arrows, highlighting, shapes, cropping and masking. Supported annotations remain editable in saved projects.

Keep your work
Save a portable project to resume later, or store it in the local library. Search, rename, duplicate and delete projects there. The storage quota and retention period are adjustable, including a no-expiration option.

Share a result
Save an image or document, copy the result or use printing. Presentation settings provide margins, orientation and pagination. Review content and metadata before sharing.

Privacy and limitations
Choose a source policy in Document settings: sanitized (default) removes URL queries and fragments; none removes sources and links; full keeps URLs without embedded credentials. This applies to saved projects and exports; library exports always use sanitized. Sources removed on save cannot be recovered. Paths, images, titles and captions may still be sensitive. Use solid masks to protect pixels; blur is only visual. Masks and crops become permanent when saved. Image files above 16 million pixels or 16,384 pixels per side are rejected before decoding; a document remains limited to 32 million pixels total.

The interface and help are available in French, English, Spanish and German. Users manage the retention and deletion of separately saved files.
