Scionos Capture 1.5.3

Local 1.5.3 preparation, unpublished. No change to the remote Store listing or public 1.5.2 links.

Capture web pages, annotate images and prepare documents locally with Scionos Capture. No account is required. Screenshots are processed on your device without sending them to a processing server.

FIVE CAPTURE MODES
Choose the visible area, a selection, a scrolling region, the whole page or an element. Adjust the bounds before confirming a selection. A cancellable delay lets you prepare a menu or a temporary page state. Browser internal pages and extension stores are protected and cannot be captured.

COMPOSE AND ANNOTATE
Import or paste multiple images, reorder them and arrange them vertically or horizontally. Add titles, captions, text, arrows, shapes, drawings and highlights. Crop images and use solid masks to protect confidential information.

RESUME YOUR WORK
Save a portable .scionos project to reopen later. Supported annotations remain editable; masks and crops become permanent when saved. The local library lets you search, rename, duplicate and delete projects. Its quota and retention period are adjustable, including a no-expiration option.

EXPORT AND SHARE
Export PNG, JPEG, WebP, HTML or Markdown/ZIP. Use your browser’s print function to save a PDF, with margin, orientation and pagination settings. Visible text collected during capture can make PDFs searchable; imported images do not provide OCR text recognition.

PRIVACY AND LIMITATIONS
Choose a source policy: sanitized removes URL queries and fragments; none removes sources and links; full keeps URLs without embedded credentials. Library exports use sanitized. URL paths, images, titles and captions may still contain sensitive information: review the result before sharing. Blur is a visual effect and does not replace a solid mask.

Imports are limited to 30 images, 16 million pixels per image and 32 million pixels in total. Print/PDF is limited to 32 million rendered pixels, 128 MiB of page images and 200 pages; a budget may be reached before the maximum page count. You control separately exported files.
